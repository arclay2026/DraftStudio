Questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. No sign-up, no email wall, no watermark. A few exclusive logos are for sale and are clearly marked with a price; everything marked “Free” is free.

Do I need Photoshop for the PSD mockups?

Photoshop works best, but you can also open PSD files for free in Photopea (photopea.com) right in your browser. Double-click the smart object layer, paste your design, and save.

I don't have Illustrator. Can I still use the logos and vectors?

Yes. Grab the SVG, EPS or PDF version instead. They open in Affinity Designer, Inkscape (free), Figma and Canva.

Do I have to credit Draft Studio?

It's never required, but it's always appreciated. A tag or a link helps us keep making free files.

Can I use a free logo as my own brand?

You can, but free logos can be downloaded by anyone, so someone else may use the same one. If you need a logo that's only yours, buy an exclusive one or ask us to draft one for you.

How do I buy an exclusive logo?

Tap “Buy on WhatsApp” on the logo's page. We confirm it's still available, you pay, and we send every file. The logo then comes off the site for good.

Can you make something specific for me?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp or send an email with what you need.
