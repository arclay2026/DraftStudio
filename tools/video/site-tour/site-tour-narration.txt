Meet Drafty Labs. One place for free design files, simple tools, and lessons that actually teach you. It's coming soon, so here's a first look at how it all works.

Part one: browse and download.

Everything starts on the home page. Type what you need, like "logo", and every match appears instantly. Filter by type, licence, orientation, and even colour. Open any design to see it up close, check its formats and licence, and download it in one click. No sign-up, and no watermark.

Part two: Canvas.

Canvas is a full design editor that runs right in your browser. Pick a size, like an Instagram post. Draw shapes, add text in over three hundred fonts, and change colours in a click. When you're done, export it as an image, ready to post.

Part three: the studios.

QR Code Studio makes codes that look designed, not generated. Choose a style, shape every dot, and it checks that your code still scans. Barcode Studio does the same for products and packaging. And Palettes gives you thirty-six ready-made colour palettes, or generates a new one, previewed on a real layout.

Part four: learn.

Design Basics is a free course in six short lessons. Press play, and the lesson reads itself to you, highlighting every word as it goes. And every lesson has its own video, right here on the channel.

That's Drafty Labs: free files, free tools, and free lessons. Coming soon. Follow along, so you're the first to know.
